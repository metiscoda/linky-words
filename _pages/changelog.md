## Version 1.0.29

- A new collection, Word Origins: 60 levels of English words grouped by the
  language they came from.
- 80 more daily puzzles, with holiday themes such as Thanksgiving, Christmas
  and New Year.
- Every new daily has its own icon.

## Versions 1.0.27 and 1.0.28

- 30 new daily puzzles, each with its own icon.
- A shared daily link shows a preview card for that day in messages and social
  apps.

## Versions 1.0.21 to 1.0.26

- Share your daily result as a grid of green and yellow squares, with a link
  that opens the same puzzle.
- Finish a category and share a summary of it, with a link that opens it for
  your friend.
- An optional reminder at 7pm when the day's puzzle is waiting.

## Versions 1.0.19 and 1.0.20

- New icons for sets, categories and daily puzzles.
- Every daily is ten levels, growing from 3&times;3 to 5&times;5 grids.
- Watching a video gives 5 hints instead of 2.

## Earlier updates, up to version 1.0.18

- The Daily Challenge: a new puzzle every day, with an archive of past days.
- The Geography collection.
- A papercraft look across the whole app, with sound, music and haptic
  feedback.

## Version 1.0

- The first release, with the Classic and Mythology collections.
- Link letters to find words in themed categories, with hints when you are
  stuck.
