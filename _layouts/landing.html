How to play

  1. 1

    Drag through the letters

    Trace a word through tiles that touch: across, down or diagonally.

  2. 2

    Every word fits the theme

    Each grid has a theme, so the words you’re hunting for are related. One day it’s Music, another it’s Pirates.

  3. 3

    Clear the grid

    Find every word to finish the level. Grids grow from 3×3 to 5×5 over the day’s ten levels, and a hint reveals a letter when you’re stuck.

Questions

Is Linky Words free?

Yes. The daily puzzle in your browser is free, with no ads and no signup. The app is free to download; it shows ads, and a single Remove Ads purchase takes them away for good.

What if I miss a day?

In the app, a missed day stays playable for six more days, then moves to the archive. In your browser, every day since the first puzzle on 1 September 2026 is in the archive.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s nothing to sign up for. The app keeps your progress on your phone. In your browser it’s saved in that browser only, so clearing its data or switching browsers starts you afresh.

Which devices can I play on?

The browser puzzle works on any phone, tablet or computer with an up-to-date browser. The app is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android.
